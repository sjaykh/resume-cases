Why a detective game?

I always wanted to be a detective. I love patterns, psychology and working things out. Maybe it comes from my late grandfather, who I’m told was a kickass officer in the CID.

My approach to product management is much the same. Most of it feels like solving a crime. So here’s my resume as the cases I’ve cracked: look at the evidence, and guess what I did.

There are more. These are the ones I remember off the top of my head. Like any detective, I forget a few, so more will turn up here as they come back to me, and as new ones land on my desk.

Maybe I’ll still end up solving crimes someday ;)Sanjay

      Five years at Entri, a vernacular edtech company in Kochi, from product intern to Director of Product. Builds AI learning products and growth systems for first-time internet users who learn in their own language, often on low-end Android phones. Works by looking under the metric: at what learners actually do, and at how the number is being measured.

      • Owns creation rate and retention as the app’s two core metrics. Ran the funnel baseline that showed signup-to-lead, not acquisition, was the larger leak, and fixed the event and cohort definitions hiding it. Case 4
